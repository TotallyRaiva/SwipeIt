Simple Setup

How to install and authorize SwipeIt on your Mac in 30 seconds.

1

Download & Install

Download SwipeIt.dmg, double click to open the installer disk image, and drag SwipeIt.app into your Applications folder.

2

Grant Accessibility

Open SwipeIt. macOS will prompt you to authorize Accessibility permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility so SwipeIt can intercept events.

3

Lock & Clean

Click the keycap icon in your macOS menu bar or press ⌃⌥⌘L to lock. Clean your keyboard safely and press ⌃⌥⌘U when finished!
